# Urgent Care Red Flags

- Chest pain with shortness of breath, fainting, jaw/arm radiation, or cold sweats should be escalated for urgent in-person care.
- Persistent high fever with confusion, dehydration, or severe weakness requires same-day clinician review.
- Severe abdominal pain, vomiting blood, black stools, or uncontrolled bleeding are emergency red flags.
- Neurological symptoms such as sudden weakness, slurred speech, or seizures should trigger emergency referral.
